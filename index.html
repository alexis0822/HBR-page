About Etienne

Etienne combines academic training with years on the sideline — working directly with professional and amateur athletes to keep them moving.

He holds a Bachelor's in Athletic Therapy from the University of Puerto Rico at Ponce, and has spent his career pairing that foundation with hands-on experience at the professional level, from BSN basketball to international soccer academies.

Every plan is built the same way: assess first, treat what's actually there, and adjust as the athlete progresses — never a one-size-fits-all program.

The things most people ask before their first session.

01What should I expect in my first session?+

A full assessment of your condition, a review of your medical history, and a conversation about your goals. From there, we build a treatment plan around you.

02How long are the sessions?+

Initial sessions run 60–90 minutes to leave room for a thorough assessment. Follow-ups are usually 45–60 minutes, depending on your plan.

03Do I need a referral?+

No referral needed. If you're already working with another provider, we're glad to coordinate with them for the best outcome.

04What should I wear?+

Comfortable athletic wear that allows a full range of motion. For some treatments, looser clothing works better — we'll let you know ahead of time.
